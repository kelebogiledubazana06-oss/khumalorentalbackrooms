About Khumalo Rental Backrooms

The Khumalo Rental Backrooms was founded in 2014 in Benoni in the Eastrand of Gauteng, by a retired policeman, Senzo Edwin Khumalo. It has been caring for communities since 2014. With more than 30 apartments we are pursuing to being one of South Africa’s leading residential property managers. Khumalo Rental Backrooms was established to provide quality, affordable, and luxurious backrooms in Benoni.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make every guest feel at home as our slogan states, with 24/7 security, free WiFi, and excellent customer service. We started with a clear mission to provide personalized, high-quality property solutions in the City of Ekurhuleni. To deliver comfortable living spaces for young working professionals, tourists, students, growing families, and business professionals while supporting the local community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be South Africa's leading residential property manager by delivering quality living at the right price. We focus on creating vibrant, secure, and well-managed communities where residents can truly thrive.
